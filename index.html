HOW TO PLAY ME!

Get started

  1. Click if you skipped the power-on screen.
  2. Press to start the sequencer. Press it again to stop.
  3. When playback is stopped, press any button to play the currently selected sound live.

Keyboard

  • Number pads 1–16: keys 1234 / QWER / ASDF / ZXCV
  • Return = PLAY  ·  5 = SOUND  ·  6 = PATTERN  ·  7 = BPM  ·  8 = FX  ·  9 = WRITE

♪ Let's make a song

The one idea

The 16 pads are two things at once: in play mode they trigger sounds, in WRITE mode the same pads are the 16 steps of the bar.

Beat #1 — boom-bap (beginner)

  1. Clear the pattern first (info drawer / PO33.clearPattern()).
  2. BPM ~90, swing ~18%.
  3. Kick: SOUND → pad 9 → SOUND. Audition pads, land on a deep thump. WRITE → steps 1, 7, 11 → WRITE.
  4. Snare (same slot 9): find a crack. WRITE → steps 5, 13 → WRITE. Each step remembers its own pad, so one drum slot holds the whole kit.
  5. Hats: a tick sound, WRITE → steps 1,3,5,7,9,11,13,15 → WRITE.
  6. PLAY. Done.

Make it groove

  • Accent: in WRITE, hold a step to latch it, nudge slider 2 up (TONE mode) — louder hit. Off-beat hats = bounce. Pull some down for ghost notes.
  • Micro-timing: latch the snare, use the on-screen timing ◀▶ (or scroll a step) to push it 6–12ms late for a laid-back feel.
  • Swing 15–22% for hip-hop, 0% for techno. Less is more — add space, not layers.

Two more beats

  • House ~124, swing 0: kick 1,5,9,13; open hat 3,7,11,15; clap 5,13; bass note on 1,9 + a higher note on 7,15 (write each pitch separately).
  • Trap ~140 half-time: kick 1,11; snare 9; hats every step, then latch a run and nudge alternate hats for rolls.

From beat to song

  1. Pattern 1 = the main loop. Get it right.
  2. Pattern 2 = a variation. Copy pattern 1 (PATTERN mode → WRITE → source → empty slot), then strip it back — drop the kick, or mute a part (hold its pad in SOUND).
  3. Pattern 3 = a fill / turnaround: busy hats, a snare roll, or a tape-stop at the end.
  4. Chain: hold PATTERN and tap e.g. 1 1 1 3   2 2 2 3 for an 8-bar loop that breathes.

Full arrangement: intro (drums, 4 bars) → groove (all, 8) → break (melody+hats, 4) → groove (8) → outro (LFO filter down, 4).

Advanced

  • Fills: latch the last few steps and lock a higher pitch or shorter TRIM length.
  • Motion-record a filter sweep: utilities → arm motion rec, PLAY, ride the FILTER slider.
  • Transitions: hold FX → 12 tape stop, 16 kill, or 5 delay into the next section.
  • Chop a break: record 1–2 bars, ✂ chop x16, tick lay out + match tempo, re-order the pads.

Choose a sound

  1. Press . Lit LEDs show which slots have a sound.
  2. Press a to pick the active slot. 1–8 are melodic (each NUMBER = a different note); 9–16 are drum (each NUMBER = a different pad).
  3. Press again to leave sound-select.

Load your own samples (Library, bottom of screen)

  1. Press then a to choose which slot you're filling. The Library says "loads into SOUND n".
  2. Pick a pack, press ▶ to preview, then click the sample — it loads onto that slot instantly.
  3. Press to exit. Now the pads (and keys) play your sample. Use it live, or WRITE it into a pattern.
  4. slot… on a row lets you target a specific slot without changing the selected one. The Slots tab shows the whole 1–16 map.

Record a sample

  1. Choose the target slot first ( + ).
  2. In the Library bar pick 🎙 microphone or 🖥 browser tab audio, then press ● record (or tap the orange pad on the unit).
  3. For tab audio: in the share dialog choose the Chrome tab playing the sound (YouTube / Spotify web) and tick “Share tab audio”. Play the part you want; press ■ stop (auto-stops at 20s).
  4. The clip lands on the selected slot and in the ★ my recordings pack. download .wav to keep it — drop it in samples/ to make it permanent.
  5. Capturing the Spotify/YouTube desktop apps isn't possible from a browser on macOS — use their web players in a tab, or route audio through a loopback device (BlackHole / Loopback) and pick it as the microphone.

Build a pattern

  1. Press to enter edit mode.
  2. Press a button (a step 1–16) to add the current sound to that step.
  3. Press again to return to performance mode, then pick a NUMBER to set the pitch / sample used when writing.

Chain patterns into a song

  1. Hold (press it to toggle the hold).
  2. Press a to select a pattern; press more numbers to append them to the chain.
  3. Press again to exit.

Tempo, swing & volume

  1. Press , then drag the two sliders (label shows what they do): swing and tempo. Mouse wheel over a slider also works.
  2. Double-tap to cycle the preset tempos (80 / 120 / 140).
  3. NUMBER buttons 1–16 set the master volume. In performance mode the sliders are pitch / volume (or filter, or trim — press to switch).

On your phone (install as an app)

  1. Open the site in a browser (see the URL your host gives you).
  2. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.   Android: menu → Install app / Add to Home screen.
  3. Launch it from the home-screen icon — runs full-screen and works offline after the first load.
  4. Everything is touch: tap pads, drag sliders. Tab-audio recording is desktop-Chrome only; mic recording works on Android and recent iOS Safari.

Record & effects

  1. While playing, hold for 3 seconds to live-record what you play into the pattern.
  2. In performance mode press to reach FLT; the dials apply a filter you can then write into a pattern.
  3. In Pattern-select mode press to copy a pattern, then choose an empty slot to paste it.
